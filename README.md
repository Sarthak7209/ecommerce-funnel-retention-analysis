# E-commerce Purchase Funnel, Cart Abandonment & Repeat Behavior Analysis

## Business problem
An online retailer sees plenty of traffic but few purchases. The product team needs to know where visitors drop off between browsing and buying, how many carts are abandoned, and what separates repeat buyers from one-time buyers, so they can decide what to fix first.

## Business questions
1. Where is the biggest drop-off in the view -> add-to-cart -> purchase funnel?
2. How long do buyers take to go from first view to purchase?
3. How many carts are abandoned, and do abandoners come back?
4. Which product categories convert best and worst?
5. What distinguishes repeat buyers from one-time buyers?

## Dataset
Retailrocket e-commerce clickstream (Kaggle): 2.7M events from May-Sep 2015.
